HomeMaster® MiniPLC is a panel-mount automation controller for residential and light commercial projects that need local I/O together with a field bus. The module combines four 24 V digital inputs, six relay outputs with a 3 A @ 250 VAC system rating per channel, four 0–10 V analog inputs, one 0–10 V analog output, RTD and 1-Wire temperature interfaces, an OLED display, microSD logging, USB-C service access, Wi-Fi, optional 10/100 Ethernet and non-isolated RS-485 Modbus RTU.

Each input channel includes TVS protection, a resettable fuse, reverse-polarity protection and EMI filtering. The RS-485 port adds transient protection, PTC limiting, a common-mode choke and fail-safe biasing; galvanic isolation is not provided. Relay drivers are optically isolated from the logic; contact rating is limited by the board design, and external fusing is required on load circuits.

The controller is listed under Made for ESPHome and ships with ESPHome pre-installed, so it integrates with Home Assistant via ESPHome YAML packages. It can poll HomeMaster Modbus expansion modules on RS-485 while serving as a Modbus slave itself. Firmware 1.1.0 uses per-device encryption keys for the native API in line with EN 18031-1 / RED Article 3(3)(d); initial pairing is accepted only during a short window after power-on.

Installation requires a qualified electrician. Mount inside a ventilated cabinet on 35 mm DIN rail. Supply 24 V DC or 85–265 V AC mains to the designated terminals only; do not apply mains to SELV terminals. The module is CE marked; hardware is published under CERN-OHL-W v2 and firmware under MIT. This pack includes the EU DoC, datasheet and user manual.
