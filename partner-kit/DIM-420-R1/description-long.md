HomeMaster® DIM-420-R1 controls two independent AC dimming channels for incandescent, halogen and dimmable LED loads on 110/230 VAC mains. Each channel supports 1 A RMS continuous current at ambient temperatures up to 40 °C with selectable leading- or trailing-edge phase control and zero-cross synchronisation for stable dimming.

Four 24 V digital inputs accept wall buttons; local hold and press actions can be configured in WebConfig alongside Modbus control. Logic power is 24 V DC SELV. The RP2350 firmware stores parameters in LittleFS. RS-485 Modbus RTU provides setpoints and status; USB-C supports configuration and UF2 firmware updates.

Mains wiring must be performed by qualified personnel following local regulations. External overcurrent protection is required. The module is a Modbus slave—integrate through a HomeMaster PLC or other master. Home Assistant visibility is typically via ESPHome on the bus controller.

Select leading or trailing edge to match the lamp technology; mismatched mode shows as flicker or limited range. Keep mains L/N routing according to the silkscreen and use an enclosed DIN panel with touch-safe covers. Document the chosen Modbus address before closing the cabinet.

Label each channel with the connected load type in the panel schedule. Confirm zero-cross timing after installation if dimming range is narrow.

CE marked with published EU Declaration of Conformity. Do not claim UL, CSA or FCC approval. Mount on DIN rail inside an enclosed panel; observe stated derating above 40 °C. The pack includes the module datasheet and EU DoC for partner resale documentation. Verify lamp compatibility on site before commissioning.
